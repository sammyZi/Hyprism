HYPRISM PRIVACY POLICY
Effective: 8 October 2026

In short: Hyprism has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no telemetry. It never sends your data to its
developers. Everything it stores stays on your PC.

1. WHAT HYPRISM STORES ON YOUR PC
   - Settings, profiles and theme palettes:  %AppData%\Hyprism
   - Backups made before Hyprism changes anything: copies of config files it edits, an export of your
     Windows personalization settings (wallpaper path, accent color, light/dark, transparency) and a copy
     of your wallpaper:  %AppData%\Hyprism\backups
   - Downloaded tools and a cache of installed-app versions:  %LocalAppData%\Hyprism
   None of this leaves your PC. Uninstalling Hyprism can delete all of it (you are asked).

2. NETWORK CONNECTIONS HYPRISM MAKES
   Hyprism only connects to the internet when you use a feature that needs it:
   - Installing, updating or checking apps: Hyprism runs Microsoft's Windows Package Manager (winget),
     which contacts Microsoft and the download servers of the app being installed. Microsoft's privacy
     statement applies: https://privacy.microsoft.com
   - Tools that are not in winget (ExplorerBlurMica, tacky-borders), and Hyprism's own update check (once a
     day, against github.com/sammyZi/Hyprism; turn it off or change it in Settings > Updates): Hyprism
     contacts GitHub (api.github.com, github.com). GitHub's privacy statement applies:
     https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies
   - Wallpapers > Discover: when you open it or search, your search words and your screen resolution are sent
     to the wallpaper source you picked, and previews and the photos you choose are downloaded from it:
       * Wallhaven (wallhaven.cc), safe-for-work results only.            https://wallhaven.cc/privacy
       * Openverse (api.openverse.org), Creative Commons images; the photo itself comes from its original
         host (for example StockSnap or Flickr).                         https://wordpress.org/about/privacy/
       * NASA Image and Video Library (images-api.nasa.gov, images-assets.nasa.gov).
                                                                           https://www.nasa.gov/privacy/
       * Lorem Picsum (picsum.photos), which serves Unsplash photos.       https://unsplash.com/privacy
     These requests are anonymous: no account or API key, no identifiers. Downloaded photos and their credit
     lines are saved in %AppData%\Hyprism\wallpapers.
   - Oh My Posh prompt themes may be read from raw.githubusercontent.com.
   - "Advanced Tweaks" opens mod pages on windhawk.net in your browser.
   Every file Hyprism downloads (tool archives, update installers, wallpapers) is scanned by the antivirus
   installed on your PC (through Windows' AMSI interface) before it is unpacked, run or saved, and refused if
   flagged. Hyprism itself sends nothing anywhere for this; your antivirus may use its own cloud service
   (for example Microsoft Defender cloud protection) according to its settings.
   Like any web request, these reveal your IP address and the "Hyprism" user agent to those servers.
   Hyprism sends no personal data, identifiers or usage information in them.

3. APPS HYPRISM INSTALLS FOR YOU
   Hyprism installs open-source apps you choose (for example TranslucentTB, Lively Wallpaper, GlazeWM,
   Zebar, Flow Launcher, PowerToys). Each is a separate program with its own license and privacy policy,
   and some collect telemetry by default (for example PowerToys and Mica For Everyone). Hyprism does not
   control or receive that data. See each project's page, linked inside Hyprism and in
   THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md in the install folder.

4. CHANGES HYPRISM MAKES TO WINDOWS
   Only when you turn a feature on: it edits the config files of the apps above, sets the desktop wallpaper,
   lock screen picture and accent color, adds sign-in (startup) entries, and may register an Explorer
   extension (with an administrator prompt). On first launch it saves a copy of your current look (and, before
   the first lock screen change, your current lock screen picture) so Settings > Restore original look, or
   uninstalling, can undo these changes.

5. CHILDREN, SALE OF DATA, TRACKING
   Hyprism collects no personal data, so it sells none, shares none and tracks no one.

6. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
   A new version of this policy ships with the Hyprism version it applies to and is shown when you
   install it.
